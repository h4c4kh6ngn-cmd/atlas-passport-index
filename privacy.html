Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Atlasify ("we", "us") publishes the passport power index and dual-nationality database at this site. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

What we collect

  • If you use the free passport scan or newsletter signup: the email address and (optionally) nationality you submit.
  • If you purchase the Mobility Dossier: your email address and payment details, handled directly by our payment processor — we do not see or store your card details ourselves.
  • Standard technical data: anonymized traffic analytics (pages visited, approximate region, device type), if and when analytics are enabled on this site.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the free scan or newsletter content you requested.
  • To deliver a digital product you purchased and handle related support.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful, in aggregate — never to identify you individually.

Who else sees it

We use third-party services to run this site — an email marketing platform to send newsletters and the free scan, and a payment processor to handle purchases. These providers only receive the data needed to perform their function (e.g. your email address, or payment details for a purchase) and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations. We do not sell your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Purchase records are kept as long as required for accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA (or anywhere with similar protections), you can ask us to:

  • Show you what data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Stop processing your data or export it elsewhere

To exercise any of these, contact us at privacy@atlasify.com. You can also unsubscribe from any email directly via the link in that email.

Cookies

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may involve a technical connection to Google's servers. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this section will be updated to name the specific tool and what it collects.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@atlasify.com.
